# Connects

A nameless connect `:{...}` or `:(...)` is the one place `{...}` stands without
a nexus of its own: it puts data into the host it follows.

# In running text

An `@` with no group after it is prose: me@example.com, or a mention of @someone.
An empty element is written with its group: a <span data-tm-kind="memo"></span> in a sentence, or a
<span data-tm-kind="memo">body</span> with content.
